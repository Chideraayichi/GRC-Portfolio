## About This Portfolio

This repository contains independent GRC (Governance, Risk, and Compliance) project work demonstrating hands-on experience in information security risk assessments, third-party vendor risk management, vulnerability management, and compliance documentation across regulated industries.

Each project was developed to professional standards — formatted as real deliverables suitable for CISO, compliance leadership, and audit review. Frameworks applied include **NIST CSF 2.0**, **NIST SP 800-53**, **NIST SP 800-161**, **HIPAA Security Rule (45 CFR Part 164)**, **HITECH**, **AICPA Trust Services Criteria (SOC 2)**, and **ISO 27001**.

## Projects

### 1. Information Security Risk Assessment — Healthcare
**Organization:** Crestview Regional Medical Center *(Fictional)*
**Framework:** NIST CSF 2.0 / HIPAA Security Rule
**File:** [Risk - Assessment - Healthcare - Crestview.docx](./Risk%20-%20Assessment%20-%20Healthcare%20-%20Crestview.docx.)

A full information security risk assessment conducted for a fictional regional medical center with 250,000+ active patients and 2,800 staff. Performed in alignment with the HIPAA Security Rule mandate under 45 CFR 164.308(a)(1)(ii)(A).

**Includes:**
- Regulatory and compliance context (HIPAA, HITECH, NIST CSF 2.0)
- Asset inventory with ePHI classification
- Threat identification matrix with HIPAA impact analysis
- Risk register with 16 identified risks (5 Critical, 5 High, 4 Medium, 2 Low)
- Every risk mapped to a specific HIPAA Security Rule citation
- Prioritized remediation roadmap with owners and timelines
- CISO-ready executive summary and compliance conclusion

### 2. Third-Party Vendor Risk Assessment — Healthcare
**Organization:** Crestview Regional Medical Center *(Fictional)*
**Vendor:** MedCloud Solutions LLC *(Fictional)*
**Framework:** HIPAA/HITECH, NIST SP 800-161
**File:** [Vendor - Risk - Assessment - MedCloud - Crestview.docx](./Vendor%20-%20Risk%20-%20Assessment%20-%20MedCloud%20-%20Crestview.docx.)

A formal vendor risk assessment initiated as a remediation action for a Critical risk identified in the Crestview healthcare risk assessment — demonstrating end-to-end GRC workflow from risk identification through vendor control evaluation and conditional approval.

**Includes:**
- Vendor tiering framework (Tier 1 Critical — direct ePHI access)
- Full vendor profile including BAA status, certifications, and subcontractor mapping
- 6-domain security questionnaire (23 control areas) covering:
  - HIPAA Compliance & Data Governance
  - Access Control & Identity Management
  - Data Protection & Encryption
  - Vulnerability Management & Patching
  - Incident Response & Business Continuity
  - Physical Security & Workforce Controls
- 6 formal findings with risk ratings, HIPAA citations, and remediation deadlines
- Overall vendor risk scorecard by domain
- Formal Conditional Approval recommendation with contractual escalation criteria

### 4. SOC 2 Type II Readiness Assessment
**Organization:** NexaCore Technologies LLC *(Fictional)*
**Framework:** AICPA Trust Services Criteria (SOC 2)
**File:** [SOC2-Readiness-Assessment-NexaCore.docx](./SOC2-Readiness-Assessment-NexaCore.docx)

A SOC 2 Type II readiness assessment evaluating organizational controls against the AICPA Trust Services Criteria. Developed to simulate the pre-audit preparation process a GRC analyst would lead prior to engaging an external auditor.

**Includes:**
- Scope definition covering Security, Availability, and Confidentiality Trust Services Categories
- Control environment evaluation across all in-scope TSC criteria
- Gap analysis identifying control deficiencies and design weaknesses
- Risk-prioritized remediation roadmap with control owners and target timelines
- Audit readiness scoring by TSC category
- Executive summary suitable for leadership and external auditor review

### 5. Vulnerability Management Program
**Organization:** NexaCore Technologies LLC *(Fictional)*
**Framework:** NIST SP 800-53
**File:** [Vulnerability-Management-Program-NexaCore.docx](./Vulnerability-Management-Program-NexaCore.docx)
